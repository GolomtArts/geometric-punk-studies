# Study 02 candidate research

Researched 2026-11-02 for Astral Lyrism’s Geometric Punk Studies. **Recommendations only: no source, title, or artwork is approved for Study 02.**

## Selection rule and verification

Prioritize the greatest number of accessory traits per original Punk, excluding character type. Study 01’s source, #8348, has seven verified traits and Male type. The next group is the official six-trait group.

The [official CryptoPunks six-attribute search](https://www.cryptopunks.app/cryptopunks/search?query=%226%20Attributes%22) returned **11 Punks Found**. Its complete ID set is 755, 1839, 2202, 3011, 4121, 5622, 5949, 6153, 7920, 7971, 8152. Each detail page was retrieved successfully and checked for its type, explicit six-attribute statement, and six distinct displayed trait names. No pagination was present for this result set. The [Larva Labs catalog](https://www.larvalabs.com/catalog/list?project=cryptopunks) and [official archive attribute-count table](https://archive.larvalabs.com/cryptopunks/attributes) also list 11 in the six-attribute group.

The table below exhausts the official filtered group as retrieved on this date. This is a reconciliation of published source records, not an independent recomputation from a versioned dataset of all 10,000 Punks. No collection-wide trait frequencies, rarity scores, or rarity rankings were calculated. All candidates have the same accessory count; artistic preference distinguishes the recommendations.

## Artistic comparison

These are curatorial judgments based on the official candidate images and verified combinations, not source facts or rarity rankings. Original head-to-neck proportions and contours take precedence over internal grid design.

| Candidate | Silhouette and coherent structure | Mondrian / De Stijl opportunity | Main constraint | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #4121 | Mohawk gives a distinct rising profile; Chinstrap and Silver Chain connect face to neck; cigarette supplies a horizontal counterweight. | Step the mohawk inside its original envelope; use a red vertical head plane, blue facial divisions, and small yellow jewelry accents. | Keep Spots individually legible and the chain attached to the original neck, not a new collar. | First recommendation: strongest contrast with Study 01’s broad top hat. |
| #8152 | Knitted Cap anchors the top; Eye Patch supplies asymmetry; Luxurious Beard defines the lower contour. | Contrast cap bands and a dark eye block with beard planes; use the Gold Chain as a small yellow neck accent. | Preserve the beard’s outline and visible neck; a large black mass could swallow Smile and jewelry. | Second recommendation: coherent complexity with a clear visual hierarchy. |
| #2202 | Straight Hair frames the head; Classic Shades and Choker establish two horizontal levels. | Use long hair divisions and broad white facial space against a blue glasses band and a small red lip plane. | Preserve the narrow neck and actual choker position; avoid turning the hair into a hat or hiding Mole and Earring. | Third recommendation: a distinct female silhouette and clear neck structure. |
| #7971 | Wild Hair contrasts with a stable Big Shades band and cigarette. | Convert hair’s irregular edge into bounded stepped planes; reserve a strong blue glasses block. | Busy hair and glasses can obscure the face and small marks. | Strong reserve for a more energetic silhouette. |
| #5622 | Blonde Bob forms a compact frame; Pipe extends the portrait laterally; Gold Chain keeps a neck focal point. | Balance a red/white hair frame with a blue eye region and small yellow chain accents. | Keep the pipe bowl distinct from the mouth and separate facial accents. | Strong reserve for horizontal extension and visible neck detail. |
| #5949 | Tassle Hat creates a pointed crown; Big Shades and Medical Mask form stacked blocks. | Exploit a vertical crown above two broad contrasting bands. | Medical Mask occludes lower-face detail; do not invent exposed Purple Lipstick to make every recorded trait visible. | Strong outline, weaker visible-trait legibility. |

| #755 | Blonde Bob provides a compact stepped silhouette; eye makeup and lipstick contrast with cigarette. | Divide hair into primary-color planes while keeping Clown Eyes Green recognizable through its shape and placement. | Small facial accents compete; changing color alone would lose the reinterpretation goal. | Clear silhouette, less structural variety than the shortlist. |
| #3011 | Vampire Hair, Small Shades, Normal Beard, and Pipe form upper, middle, lower, and lateral anchors. | Use hair steps, a narrow eye band, and a horizontal pipe against beard planes. | Frown, pipe, and beard need careful separation at the mouth. | Good portrait architecture, but another bearded male follows Study 01 more closely. |
| #6153 | Messy Hair, Horned Rim Glasses, Medical Mask, and Clown Nose produce dense overlapping face regions. | Translate glasses and mask into intersecting planes while keeping the hair contour. | Do not expose an invented beard under the mask; six recorded traits need not all be visible. | Technically interesting, less readable at a small gallery size. |

## Three recommendations — approval pending

1. **CryptoPunk #4121:** prioritize the mohawk, original profile, Chinstrap, neck, and Silver Chain. Its vertical silhouette and horizontal cigarette offer a strong alternative to #8348’s top-hat structure.
2. **CryptoPunk #8152:** prioritize cap width, eye-patch asymmetry, beard perimeter, and neck/chain relationship. Separate dark facial hair from primary-color structural planes.
3. **CryptoPunk #2202:** prioritize the straight-hair perimeter, face proportions, shades, and narrow neck with Choker. It offers a quieter geometric framework and a clear departure from Study 01.

These are alternative references for one artwork, not approved assignments or final titles. Before creating artwork, obtain the artist’s source choice and confirm the full original image, head-to-neck proportions, and locations of all distinguishing marks. Recompose internal planes and negative spaces while retaining the source silhouette; do not simply recolor source pixels or enlarge the neck to fit a grid.
